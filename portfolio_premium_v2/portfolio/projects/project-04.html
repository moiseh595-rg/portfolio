THE CONCEPT

A story told through a website.

Built for SAÉ 1.05 during my computer science studies, this website introduces a medieval escape game centered on a castle, an alchemist and a legendary sword.

Visitors explore the setting, four story chapters and practical information. This is a promotional showcase for the escape game, not a browser-playable escape game.

STORY JOURNEY

Four chapters. One legend.

CHAPTER 01

Awakening in the castle

A mysterious sword transports the visitors into the past. The countdown begins.

CHAPTER 02

The alchemy storeroom

Symbols and clues lead the way toward a hidden jewel.

CHAPTER 03

The alchemist’s workshop

Collected objects and ingredients help restore a broken jewel.

CHAPTER 04

The final confrontation

The three jewels reveal the legendary sword and an ancestor’s secret.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Building the web experience together.

This was a collaborative project with ******** (Group 8). My main contributions were the HTML structure, header, navigation, footer, writing stages 1 and 2, integrating illustrations and parts of the CSS layout.

My teammate contributed the overall visual style, general CSS, stages 3 and 4, and image creation. The illustrations were AI-generated using tools including ChatGPT and Gemini.

I practiced semantic HTML, Flexbox, dropdown navigation, responsive layouts and teamwork in web development.
